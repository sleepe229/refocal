# Privacy policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-17

## In short

Refocal processes your photos **only on your device**. We do not collect, store or send your
photos or any personal data. There are no servers, accounts, ads, analytics or crash reports.

## Data on your device

- **Photos** you pick are processed on the device. Working copies are kept in the app's private
  storage and deleted automatically after 7 days at most; you can delete them sooner in
  Settings → Storage.
- **History** (the settings used and small previews) is kept on the device. You can turn it off
  and clear it in Settings.
- **The app log** (technical events, without file paths or photo contents) stays on the device.
  It leaves the device only if you export it yourself.

## Network connections

Refocal connects to the internet **only** when you:

1. open the model list or download a model: files come from this project's GitHub releases
   (GitHub receives your IP address under its own privacy policy);
2. turn on the check for model updates (off by default; at most once every 7 days);
3. tap "Report a problem" or "Report a bad result": a GitHub page opens in your browser with a
   pre-filled text that you can read and edit before sending. The photo is not included. You can
   share it separately, by your own action, after a warning.

Requests carry no identifiers, cookies or advertising IDs, and use HTTPS only.

## Photo metadata

By default the location (GPS) is removed from results, and a mark that the image was edited with
AI (IPTC DigitalSourceType) is added. Both can be changed in Settings.

## Permissions

- Refocal does **not** ask for access to your whole gallery: you choose photos with the system
  picker.
- On Android, Refocal may ask to show notifications, for the progress of long or batch jobs.

## Children

Refocal collects no data about anyone, including children.

## Deleting data

All data stays on the device. It is removed when you uninstall the app, or in
Settings → Storage.
