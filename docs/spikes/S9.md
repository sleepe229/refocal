## Step 1: FBCNN

FBCNN predicts the JPEG quality factor (QF) and conditions the restoration on it. Per-tile
predictions would differ and show at the seams, so the pack has two graphs: `estimate` (tile →
QF; the core averages it over a few tiles) and `restore` (tile, QF → tile).
`tools/models/convert_fbcnn.py` (run by `convert.yml`) converts both and writes golden pairs and
`qf_report.json`: how far the per-tile QF strays from the whole-image one at qualities 10–90.

Next: the core takes the two-graph pack as a stage before SR (a 1× pass, tiles multiple of 8),
the "jpeg" golden category, and timings on the S24 (T2).
